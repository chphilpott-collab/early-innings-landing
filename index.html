I played 4 years of high school baseball at Bethesda Chevy Chase, then 4 more years of college ball. I've developed multiple athletes into standout players. I coach Little League and travel tournament teams, and I run private lessons.

But here's the real story. I was undersized as a player. I know firsthand that kids develop at different rates. Team practices push forward the already-best players and leave everyone else behind. That's what I'm here to fix.
